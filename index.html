How the road is laid

The game stands at v0.121.0 live, the first5 tutorial at v0.15.0 in its sandbox, TestFlight build 1.0 (1) up since 8/18 with the listing, age rating and ten screenshots already entered in App Store Connect. What stands between here and the store is not one big thing but a stack of small ones, plus the two that only Wyatt's Mac can do (archive build 2, press Submit).

The shape: two weeks of content (Oct 17–30) · content freeze Fri Oct 30 · build 2 + privacy labels + pricing on Wyatt's Mac Oct 31 · TestFlight soak over the weekend · Submit Mon Nov 2 · Apple's review usually lands in 24–48 h · release aim Thu Nov 5 · fallback Thu Nov 12 if the review bounces once. The second week is kept deliberately empty of new content so a rejection has somewhere to land.

After the release (not on the road, so it stays honest)

These are the pieces the plan deliberately leaves for 1.0.1 and beyond, each a card or a round on its own: the store (Patron, themes, tip jar — needs the StoreKit bridge and a new shell build, so it is its own TestFlight cycle), push notifications (same reason), the full Living Sky waves beyond wave one, the Where You Stand ranking + honours redesign, the Rising Ladder epic tier, seasons (zodiac-month or quarterly), the Second Night campaign and the 40 unauthored constellations as new beasts.
